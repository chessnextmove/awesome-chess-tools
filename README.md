## Play Online

- [Lichess](https://lichess.org) - Free and open-source internet chess server with puzzles, analysis and tournaments.
- [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) - The largest chess community in the world; play, learn and watch.
- [FICS (Free Internet Chess Server)](https://www.freechess.org) - One of the oldest internet chess servers, completely free.

## Analysis & Move Calculators

- [Chess Next Move - Free Chess Move Calculator](https://chessnewmove.com/) - Free online tool that finds the best move in any chess position with instant engine analysis.
- [Lichess Analysis Board](https://lichess.org/analysis) - Free analysis board powered by Stockfish running in your browser.
- [Chess.com Analysis](https://www.chess.com/analysis) - Analyze your games with engine evaluations and mistake review.

## Chess Engines

- [Stockfish](https://stockfishchess.org) - The strongest open-source chess engine, free for everyone.
- [Leela Chess Zero](https://lczero.org) - Neural-network chess engine and distributed computing project.

## Training & Puzzles

- [ChessTempo](https://chesstempo.com) - Online chess tactics training and endgame practice.
- [Chessable](https://www.chessable.com) - Courses that use science-backed spaced repetition to boost retention.
- [OpeningTrainer](https://openingtrainer.com) - Train openings by playing simulated games against the Lichess database.
- [EndgameTrainer](https://endgametrainer.com) - 6000+ high-quality theoretical endgame positions.

## Learning Resources

- [ChessBase](https://en.chessbase.com) - News, reports and interviews from the chess world.
- [Chessgames.com](https://www.chessgames.com) - Online PGN game database and chess learning community.
- [Chess Programming Wiki](https://www.chessprogramming.org) - Repository of information about programming computers to play chess.

## Game Databases

- [365Chess](https://www.365chess.com) - One of the biggest online chess game databases.
- [Chess-Results](https://chess-results.com) - The international chess tournament results server.
